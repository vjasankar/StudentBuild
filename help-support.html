Learn how StudentBuild technical support works and find answers to your questions.

1. Project Submission

Students can submit project ideas with descriptions, department, domain, project type, budget range, and expected deadline.

🔍

2. Project Review

Our technical admin team carefully reviews your submission requirements to evaluate technical feasibility and scope.

💬

Project Status Guide

In Review / Submitted

Accepted
Your project request has been accepted for development and technical guidance!
Needs Details
The technical team requires more details regarding your features, hardware, or software requirements. Use messages to clarify.
Rejected
Your submission could not be accepted. Review team notes or message support to explore alternative project options.
